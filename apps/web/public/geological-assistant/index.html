Preparing for the Kerala PSC Geological Assistant exam

Geological Assistant Prep is made for candidates of the Kerala PSC Geological Assistant recruitment in the Ground Water Department (Category No. 46/2011). The technical paper tests degree-level geology across five units: geotectonics and geomorphology, structural geology and stratigraphy, mineralogy, petrology, and hydrogeology.

The cards and questions are written from Kerala PSC previous question papers for geology posts, including Junior Hydrogeologist, Assistant Geologist and HSST Geology, along with UPSC Geo-Scientist and CGWB hydrogeologist papers. So the same revision is useful if you are also writing other Kerala PSC geologist exams.
