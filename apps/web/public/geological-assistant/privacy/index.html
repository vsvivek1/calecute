Privacy Policy

Geological Assistant Prep · Effective 10 October 2026

Geological Assistant Prep is made by Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited (“we”). Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the data fiduciary for the information below. Contact: info@calecutech.com.

In short

  • We collect only what is needed to sign you in, unlock your purchase and plan your revision.
  • No ads. No third-party tracking or analytics. No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • You can delete everything at any time.

2. What we do not collect

Location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, phone number, advertising ID. No advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How we use it

Only for the purposes above. We do not sell data and do not build marketing profiles.

4. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service.
  • Google provides Google Sign-In.
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited processes payments under its own privacy policy. Checkout opens on Razorpay’s page, and card or UPI details go to Razorpay only.

We share data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete it we remove your progress and profile from the live database. We keep a minimal payment record (order ID, amount, date) for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Backups are overwritten in the normal cycle.

6. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database rules let each user read only their own records. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or complain. Email info@calecutech.com, or follow Delete account. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email.
