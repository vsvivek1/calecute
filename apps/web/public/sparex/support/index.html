Common questions

I didn't get the SMS code.

Check the number includes the right digits, wait a minute and tap resend. If it still doesn't arrive, sign in with Google instead or email us.

How do I remove a listing once the part is sold?

Open My account, find the listing and delete it. Logging it as sold keeps it in your private sales record.

A seller cheated me.

Email us the listing and what happened. We will investigate and remove sellers who commit fraud. For money already paid, also report it to your bank or the police.

How do I get Premium?

Tap Request Premium on the pricing page. We reply by email with how to pay and switch it on once payment arrives.
