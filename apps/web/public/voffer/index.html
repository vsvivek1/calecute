How it goes down

1

Spot it

See live offers near you, sorted by distance. Filter by category or search.

2

Grab it

Reserve one or more units before the stock runs out or the offer expires.

3

Show it

At the shop, show your code or QR. Pay the shop directly, the way you normally would.

4

Follow it

Follow your favourite shops and get an alert when they post something new.

Got a shop?

Set up your shop with address, map pin, hours and phone. Post an offer with price, usual price, stock and expiry, then scan customers' codes to mark orders redeemed. Voffer takes no cut of the sale.
