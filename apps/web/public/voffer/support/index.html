Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder, then try signing up again. If it still doesn't arrive, email us.

The shop wouldn't accept my code.

Check the offer hasn't expired and you're at the shop that posted it. Then email us the code and shop name.

How do I pay?

At the shop, when you show your code. Voffer never takes payment.

I'm a firm. How do I mark an order as collected?

Open the redeem screen and scan the customer's QR code, or type their six-letter code.

Why don't I see offers near me?

Allow location for Voffer, or pick your city manually.
