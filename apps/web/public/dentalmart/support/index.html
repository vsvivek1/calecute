Common questions

An order hasn't arrived.

Check its status in Orders. If it's late, contact the seller; if you get no answer, email us with the order number.

Can I buy as an individual?

No. DentalMart is only for clinics and licensed dental professionals.

How do sellers get paid?

Through Stripe Connect, set up during seller onboarding. Payouts go straight to your bank account from Stripe.

Why do I see the same product from several sellers?

Each seller makes its own offer. Compare price, minimum order and lead time.

How do I return a product?

Returns follow the seller's terms. Contact the seller from the order page.
