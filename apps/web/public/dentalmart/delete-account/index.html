Delete your account

DentalMart (com.calecutech.dentalmart)

You can delete your DentalMart account and the personal data linked to it at any time. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open DentalMart and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the confirmation, and tap Delete account again.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. If you still have orders in progress (as a seller, orders to fulfil; as a buyer, paid orders on their way), the app tells you so; finish or cancel those first.

Or ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, have a seller account, or prefer not to use the app, email us and we will delete the account for you.

  1. Write to info@calecutech.com with the subject "DentalMart account deletion".
  2. Include the email address you sign in with and your clinic or company name, so we can find the right account.
  3. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile.
  • Your clinic or company profile, licence number and tax ID.
  • Saved delivery addresses and your cart.
  • For sellers: your listings that have never been sold.

What we keep, and why

Orders and invoices are kept for 8 years because tax law requires it. Your name, email, phone, licence number and tax ID are removed from them, and delivery addresses keep only the city, state, postal code and country. Stripe keeps its own records of payments and seller identity checks under its policy.

Delete some data without closing the account

Email us and say what you want removed; we will delete it and keep the rest of the account.

Need help with an order or your account?

Write to us and we usually reply within two working days.
