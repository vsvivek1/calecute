How it works

Owner adds buses

A bus owner registers free, adds each bus with its route and stops, and sends the driver a link.

Driver taps Start

The driver opens the link in the app and taps Start. Location keeps sharing with the screen off until they tap Stop.

Riders see it live

Anyone can search a stop or route and see each bus on the map. No sign-up needed.

Private by design

  • No ads
  • No rider accounts
  • Rider location never collected
  • Driver location only while sharing
