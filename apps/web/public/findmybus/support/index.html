Common questions

My bus isn't showing on the map

The driver must tap Start in the app. A bus that hasn't sent a position for 5 minutes shows as not live.

Location stops when the screen is off

Use the Android app, not the browser, and allow location when asked. If you denied it, tap Open settings on the driver screen and allow location.

I lost my owner dashboard link

Email us from the email you registered with and we will send it again.
