Privacy Policy

Find My Bus (com.calecutech.findmybus) · Effective 4 October 2026

Find My Bus is made by Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Find My Bus Android app and its website. For India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the data fiduciary for the information described here. Contact: info@calecutech.com.

In short

  • Riders: we collect nothing. No account, and your own location is never collected.
  • Drivers: the phone's location is shared only between tapping Start and Stop, with a notification showing the whole time.
  • Bus owners: contact details and the buses and routes you add.
  • No ads. No selling of data. No third-party tracking or analytics.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Driver location: latitude, longitude, speed and direction, about every 10 seconds, only while the driver has tapped Start. In the Android app this continues with the screen off; a notification is shown the whole time. Only the latest position of each bus is kept.To show riders where the bus is now. This is the app's core function.
Bus owner details: name, phone number, optional company name, email and town.To run the owner account and contact the owner about it.
Bus details: registration number, bus name, route and stops entered by the owner.To show the bus to riders and match searches.
Waitlist: name, phone, email, town and notes typed into the waitlist form.To tell you when Find My Bus reaches your route.

Not collected: rider location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or advertising IDs. Location is never collected in the background except while a driver is actively sharing.

2. Permissions

  • Precise location (drivers only): to share the bus's position after tapping Start.
  • Foreground service (location): to keep sharing with the screen off, with a visible notification, until Stop.
  • Notifications: to show that sharing notification.

3. Who can see it

A bus's latest position, registration number, name, route, stops and operator name are shown publicly to riders on the map. Owner contact details, waitlist entries and driver links are never shown publicly.

Data is stored with our service providers, who process it only to run Find My Bus: Supabase (database) and Vercel (website hosting). Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Only the latest location of each bus is stored; each new position replaces the last.
  • Removing a bus deletes it and its location immediately.
  • Owner and waitlist details are kept until you ask us to delete them.

5. Your rights and deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. See Delete your account, or email info@calecutech.com. We act on requests within 30 days.

6. Children

Find My Bus is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
