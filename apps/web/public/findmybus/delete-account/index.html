Delete your account and data

App: Find My Bus (com.calecutech.findmybus) · Developer: Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited

Riders have no account and nothing to delete. Bus owners and waitlist members can delete their data at any time.

Remove a bus

  1. Open your owner dashboard link.
  2. Tap Remove next to the bus.

The bus, its route and its location are deleted at once.

Delete your whole owner account or waitlist entry

  1. Email info@calecutech.com from the email you registered with, or include the phone number you registered with.
  2. Subject: Delete my Find My Bus account
  3. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your owner record: name, phone, company, email, town.
  • All your buses, routes, stops and their locations.
  • Your driver links stop working.
  • Your waitlist entry, if any.

What is kept

Nothing, except backup copies, which are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
