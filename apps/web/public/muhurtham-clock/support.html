Support

Muhurtham Clock · Calecute Technologies

Write to support@calecutech.com. Tell us your town, the date you were looking at and what you expected to see; a screenshot helps. We answer within two working days.

Common questions

The clock shows "offline" or does not load. The first load of a day needs internet. Once loaded, that day works offline until the next sunrise. If it still fails on a connection, our server may be down; try again in a few minutes and let us know if it persists.

The times do not match my printed panchangam. See the questions on the home page: the app uses your town's own sunrise rather than a reference town's, and printed tables are often rounded.

I think a window is wrong. Please tell us. Every window carries a source line in the app; quote it with the date and town and we will check the computation against the convention it names.

My town is missing. Pick the nearest town for now, and send us the name; the list is ours to extend.

Refunds. Google Play handles refunds for the purchase under its own policy; within the first two hours you can refund from the Play Store directly. After that, write to us and we will ask Google on your behalf.
