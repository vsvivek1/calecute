Last updated: 18 October 2026

This page matches the Data safety section of the app's Google Play listing.

Summary

  • Data collected: none.
  • Data shared with third parties: none.
  • Account or sign-in: none.
  • Ads, analytics, crash reporting: none.
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone: never requested.

Google Play Data safety answers

  • Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? No.
  • Is all user data encrypted in transit? Not applicable, because no user data is collected.
  • Can users request that their data be deleted? Not applicable, because no user data is collected. Uninstalling the app removes its settings from the phone.

What stays on your phone

A few settings are saved on the phone only: start day, language, story text on or off, home or lock screen choice, and the last day the wallpaper changed. They never leave the phone.

Network use

Paintings and stories are bundled in the app. The app may contact Wikimedia Commons only to download a painting that is not bundled. No personal information is sent.
