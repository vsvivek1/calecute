Common questions

My chart looks different from my astrologer's.

Check the birth time and place, and the ayanamsa setting in Settings. Kerala astrologers mostly use Lahiri; others use different ones.

I don't know the exact birth time.

Set the time accuracy on the profile. Readings that depend on the exact lagna are marked as less certain.

Can I see the chart in Malayalam?

Yes. Change the language in Settings or on each profile.

How do I stop the daily notification?

Turn it off in Settings, under notifications, or in your phone's settings.

How do I share a chart with family?

Open the profile and create a share link. You can revoke it at any time.
