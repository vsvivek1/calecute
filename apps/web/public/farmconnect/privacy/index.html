Privacy Policy

FarmConnect (com.calecutech.farmconnect) · Effective 7 October 2026

FarmConnect is made by Calecute Technologies LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the FarmConnect Android app and this website. For India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the data fiduciary for the information described here. Contact: info@calecutech.com.

In short

  • You sign in with Google. We keep your name, email and photo from Google, plus the phone, district and farm details you add.
  • Listings, events and help-desk answers you post can be seen by other users.
  • Payments go through Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • On the website, your location is used once to find your district, and only the district is kept.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no third-party tracking.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Account: your Google name, email address and profile photo.To sign you in and show who you are to people you trade with.
Profile: role (farmer, buyer, both or officer), phone number, district, panchayat, organisation name, a short bio, and the crops you grow with their acreage.So buyers can find and contact you, and officers can help you.
Listings: title, description, price, quantity, harvest date, district and a photo.To show your produce to buyers.
Orders: what was ordered, quantity, total, status and times, and Razorpay's order and payment references.To complete the sale, and keep the record the law requires.
Community: events you create or attend, questions you ask and officers' answers, and your crop task schedule.To run those features.
Location (website only): your browser's position, when you tap to detect your district. Only the district is saved.To fill in your district for you.
Voice (website only): the text of what you say when you use voice listing. Your browser turns speech into text; we send the text to an AI service and do not store it.To fill in the listing form for you.

Not collected: your contacts, call logs, SMS, precise location history, advertising IDs, or card, UPI or bank details. We never receive your Google password.

2. Permissions

  • Internet: to load and post listings.
  • Camera and photos (through your phone's own picker, only when you add a photo): to attach a picture to a listing.
  • On the website, your browser may ask for location and microphone, only when you tap the district or voice buttons.

3. How we use it

  • To show listings, take orders and connect buyers with sellers.
  • To let officers answer farmers' questions.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use your data to train AI models.

4. Who we share it with

Only the service providers below, each processing data for us under its own privacy terms. We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

  • Google Sign-In: signing you in (privacy policy)
  • Razorpay: taking payments and refunds (privacy policy)
  • Vercel: hosting the service and storing listing photos (privacy policy)
  • Neon: our database (privacy policy)
  • Anthropic: turning a spoken listing into form fields (privacy policy)
  • Google Maps Platform and OpenStreetMap: turning a location into a district (privacy policy)

5. What other users can see

Other users can see your name, photo, district, organisation and the listings, events and help-desk posts you make. A buyer and seller see each other's contact details for an order between them. Listing photos are stored at public web addresses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. Order and payment records are kept for 8 years because Indian tax law requires it. Everything else is deleted when you delete your account.

7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Your app sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to info@calecutech.com. We reply within 30 days. To delete your account, see Delete your account. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

FarmConnect is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
