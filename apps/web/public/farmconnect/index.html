How it works

01

List your harvest

Add the crop, price, quantity, harvest date and a photo. On the web you can speak it in Malayalam or English.

02

Buyers find it

Buyers filter by crop and district and request the stock they want.

03

Pay safely

Stock is held for 15 minutes while the buyer pays by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay.

04

Grow together

Events, a crop task schedule, and a help desk where agricultural officers answer questions.
