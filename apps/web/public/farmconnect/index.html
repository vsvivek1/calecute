Farmers' digital mall

Your own shop, inside FPO

Every farmer can open a shop in FPO. Buyers browse all the farmer shops together in one mall, by district, and order straight from the farm.

  • Open your shop in minutes. Choose a shop name, write a short description, and add a photo of your farm or stall.
  • Stock it with your produce. Every harvest you list shows up in your shop, with your price and quantity.
  • Found by district. Buyers pick their district and see every farmer's shop near them in one place.
  • Straight from the farm. No middleman between your field and your buyer.

How it works

From harvest to doorstep

Four simple steps, the same on your phone and on the web.

01

List your harvest

Add the crop, price, quantity, harvest date and a photo. On the web you can speak it in Malayalam or English.

02

Open your shop

Give your shop a name, a short description and a photo. Your listings appear in it, inside the farmers' mall.

03

Buyers order

Buyers browse shops by district, pick what they want and request the stock straight from your farm.

04

Pay safely

Stock is held for 15 minutes while the buyer pays by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay.
