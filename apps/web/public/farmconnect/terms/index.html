1. What FarmConnect is

FarmConnect is a marketplace where farmers and farmer producer organisations list produce and buyers order it. The seller sets the price and is responsible for the produce: its quality, quantity, and handover. Agricultural officers on the help desk give general guidance, not professional advice for which we are responsible.

2. Accounts

You must be 18 or over. Give true details about yourself, your farm and your organisation, and keep your Google account secure.

3. Selling

  • List only produce you have, at the quantity and quality you describe, with a real photo.
  • Follow food-safety and agricultural laws that apply to what you sell.
  • Hand over what a buyer has paid for, as agreed.

4. Buying and payment

When you request stock it is held for 15 minutes while you pay through Razorpay. If you don't pay in time, the hold lapses and you can cancel the request. If your payment arrives after the stock has gone, it is refunded in full automatically. For any other problem with an order, contact the seller first and then us; we will help resolve it and refund where the seller cannot deliver.
