Delete your account

FarmConnect (com.calecutech.farmconnect)

You can delete your FarmConnect account and the personal data linked to it at any time. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete in the app

  1. Open FarmConnect and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account, just under Sign out.
  3. Read the confirmation and tap Delete. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

You can also do this on the web: sign in and open Profile, then use Delete account at the bottom of the page.

Or ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, or prefer not to, email us and we will delete the account for you.

  1. Write to info@calecutech.com with the subject "FarmConnect account deletion".
  2. Include the Google email address you sign in with, so we can find the right account.
  3. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile, including phone, district, farm and crop details.
  • Your listings and their photos.
  • Your events, RSVPs, help-desk questions and crop schedule.

What we keep, and why

Records of completed orders and payments (amount, date, product and Razorpay reference) are kept for 8 years, because Indian tax law requires it. They are kept only for that purpose, with your name removed where the law allows. Razorpay keeps its own payment records under its policy.

Delete some data without closing the account

Email us and say what you want removed; we will delete it and keep the rest of the account.
