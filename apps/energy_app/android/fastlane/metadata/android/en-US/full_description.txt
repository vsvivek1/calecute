Prepare for the BEE National Certification Examination for Energy Managers and Energy Auditors without planning a thing. Recallio plans every day for you, counted back from your exam date, and shows one small card at a time at the study times you choose.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set your exam date and the papers you are taking. The app decides what to study each day, including revision.
• At each study time you get an alert: "10 cards, about 6 minutes". Open it and study one card at a time.
• After each card, tap one of four honest buttons: Didn't get it, Tough, Understood or So simple. The card comes back just before you would forget it.
• Tough cards come back sooner; easy ones move far away, so your time goes where it is needed.
• At noon, one key fact from your papers arrives as a short flash.
• The last weeks switch to revision, and the last two days to cram mode with summaries and key facts.
• A weekly report shows what became easy and what is still tough.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• All four papers: General aspects of energy management and energy audit; Energy efficiency in thermal utilities; Energy efficiency in electrical utilities; Energy performance assessment for equipment and utility systems.
• Over 2,000 cards written from the BEE guide books, in chapter order, each naming the page it came from.
• Worked numericals one step at a time, with the reason for every step.
• Read-aloud on every card, English and Malayalam app language, works offline.
• Sign in with Google to use the same progress on your phone and on the web at energy.recallio.calecutech.com.

PRICING
The first 20 cards of every paper are free. Unlock a paper for ₹1,000 (one-time, no subscription) to study all of it until your exam. Coupon codes are also accepted.
