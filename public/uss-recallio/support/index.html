Common questions

How do I change the language?

Use the language switch in the app, or Me → Settings. Malayalam and English subject cards always stay in their own language.

How do I turn reminders off or change the time?

Me → Settings → Reminders.

Can I add a second child?

Each account supports one child. A second child needs a second parent account.

I paid but the Season Pass did not unlock.

Check that you are signed in with the same Google account you paid with, then email us your Google Play order number (starts with GPA.). See Refunds.

I found a mistake in a card.

Thank you. Email us a screenshot and what you think is wrong. We check every report against the textbook.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete account.
